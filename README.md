## 🌟 Overview

**Global Neon Pulse** is a high-performance, vibrant web application that brings every timezone on Earth to your screen. Built with a focus on **Glassmorphism** and **Cyberpunk aesthetics**, it’s not just a clock—it’s a piece of digital art for your workspace.

> "Precision meets Neon." 💎

---


## ✨ Features

* 🌍 **Universal Coverage:** Access timezones for every country and major city across the globe using the `Intl` API.
* 🌓 **Format Toggle:** Seamlessly switch between **12-hour (AM/PM)** and **24-hour (Military)** formats.
* 🎨 **Neon Glassmorphism:** A stunning UI with backdrop-blur effects and animated radial glow orbs.
* 📱 **Responsive Design:** Optimized for 4K monitors down to the smallest mobile screens.
* ⚡ **Zero Lag:** Highly optimized JavaScript for smooth, battery-efficient second-by-second updates.
